**SBSense** is a **real-time data collection and analysis application** developed for the **UTSA Biomedical Engineering Lab**, designed to streamline experimental workflows and enhance data processing efficiency. The tool enables researchers to **capture, visualize, and analyze high-resolution data** from biomedical experiments in real time, reducing manual effort and improving accuracy. Built in **MATLAB**, it integrates **multithreaded execution** and **event-handling pipelines** to ensure smooth performance during data-intensive tasks. Key features include **weighted object detection** (leveraging curve fitting for validation), **drag-and-drop visual cropping**, and **real-time image and plot display**, allowing users to configure inputs dynamically and export results in **image or CSV formats**. The system’s **custom queue pipeline** optimizes resource management, ensuring responsiveness even with large datasets.

The project demonstrates expertise in **signal processing, GUI development, and algorithm optimization**, making it particularly valuable for **biomedical research, diagnostic imaging, and experimental automation**. By providing **intuitive visualization and export capabilities**, SBSense bridges the gap between raw data acquisition and actionable insights, accelerating research in **neurodegenerative studies, implant testing, and physiological monitoring**. Its modular design and **user-friendly interface** reflect a focus on **practical usability** while maintaining robust technical performance.

This tool highlights skills in **software engineering, digital signal processing, and real-time systems**—critical for roles in **biomedical technology, research instrumentation, and data-driven healthcare solutions**.
