#### **Thesis Overview**
*Colpa di Fulmine* is a **multidisciplinary thesis** by Stanley Oliver Soo, combining **original text, music, and multilingual sources** to explore themes of **identity, doubt, and societal polarization**. The title, an Italian phrase meaning *"fault of lightning,"* merges the concepts of **love (colpo di fulmine, "love at first sight")** and **transgression (colpa, "fault")**, framing love as both a revelation and a moral conflict.

#### **Core Themes**
- **Internal Conflict**: The work depicts the speaker’s struggle with **self-doubt, sexuality, gender identity, and existential meaning** in a polarized world.
- **Truth and Deception**: Questions how one discerns truth when **personal and societal narratives clash**, particularly in **religious, ideological, and identity-based debates**.
- **Multilingual Patchwork**: The text integrates:
  - Original prose and poetry.
  - Excerpts from **instant-messaging conversations** (reflecting real-life dialogues).
  - **Biblical passages** (e.g., Romans 1, Matthew 7).
  - Lyrics from *The Battle Hymn of the Republic* (symbolizing judgment and divine wrath).

#### **Musical Composition**
- **Original Score**: Orchestrated for **strings, winds/brass, piano, and synthesizer**, with **VST MIDI automation** for dynamic control.
- **Folk Influences**: Themes draw from **French and Italian folk traditions**, reinforcing cultural and emotional layers.
- **Motivic Structure**: Repetition and permutation of musical motifs mirror the **psychological and philosophical tensions** in the text.
- **Text-Music Interaction**: The score **reinterprets and amplifies** the text’s themes, using **instrumentation to evoke specific emotions** (e.g., brass for judgment, warmer tones for vulnerability).

#### **Key Contributions (Resume Highlights)**
- **Text**:
  - Wrote **original multilingual content** (English, Italian, French).
  - Strategically **interwove quotations** to add depth.
  - Performed a **dramatic reading** for the final piece.
- **Music**:
  - Composed **thematic material** informed by folk traditions.
  - Orchestrated for a **hybrid ensemble** (acoustic + electronic).
  - Developed a **system to encode Italian words as pitch-class sets** for atonal passages.

#### **Significance**
*Colpa di Fulmine* is a **portrait of existential and ideological struggle**, using **artistic innovation** to challenge perceptions of truth, identity, and morality. It appeals to audiences in **literature, music composition, and interdisciplinary studies**, showcasing **creative rigor and technical skill** in both text and score.
